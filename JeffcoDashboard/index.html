    Strategic Sorting uses decision thresholds to categorize schools into strategy groups. Adjust the sliders below to change how schools are evaluated; outcomes appear in the results tables at the bottom (Strategic Decision Summary and Decision by School tabs).
    The flowchart allows you to visualize the decision logic dynamically by school.     Flow 1: Decision Routing
    • Enrollment Thresholds by School Level (2025-26)
      Minimum enrollment thresholds for Elementary, K-8, Middle, High, and 6-12 schools (driven by Decision Data Export enrollment)
    • Current Utilization Threshold (25-26)
      Minimum percentage of capacity used before a school is considered underutilized (0-100%, default: 60%)
    • High Utilization Threshold (25-26)
      Percentage threshold for identifying highly utilized or overcrowded schools (0-120%, default: 90%)
    • Enrollment Growth Threshold
      Minimum expected change in student enrollment (-50% to +50%, default: 0%)
    • Distance to Nearby Schools by School Level
      Distance thresholds (miles, varies by school level) used to determine whether nearby schools are within a “reasonable” distance
     Flow 2: Expansion
    • Attendance Area Enrollment (2024)
      Percentage threshold for attendance area enrollment (60-100%, default: 80%)
    • Composite Building Score
      Building condition score threshold (1-10 scale, default: 5)
    • Educational Adequacy
      Minimum percentage threshold for educational adequacy (0-100%, default: 50%)
     Flow 3: Maintenance/Investment
    • Composite Building Score Above/Below Threshold
      Building condition score thresholds (1-10 scale, default: 5)
     Flow 4: Closure/Consolidation
    • Composite Building Score
      Building condition score threshold (1-10 scale, default: 5)
    • Distance to Welcoming Schools
      Distance thresholds by school level (miles)
     Prioritization within Strategy Sorting lets you prioritize schools within a strategy group using weighted criteria.

    Controls panel (Left):
    Use the weight sliders (0–10, 0.5 increments). You can also disable a metric using its checkbox. Higher weights increase that metric’s influence on the priority score.

    Results tables (Bottom):
    Open the Strategy Prioritization tab to select strategy groups and view the prioritized list. Each group row has a ▸ arrow that opens a flyout where you can optionally filter to specific outcomes within that group.

    Table display:
    Use the “Show values / Show scores” toggle above the table to switch between the raw values (%, miles, counts) and the normalized 0–100 metric scores. This toggle does not change the ranking.

    Note: Priority scores are normalized and shown in the table; map markers are not resized by priority.     Results tables at the bottom display outcomes from Strategic Sorting and Prioritization.

    Strategic Decision Summary: Count of schools by decision type (strategy group).
    Decision by School: Each school with its assigned decision type.
    Strategy Prioritization: Ranked lists within a strategy group (Step 4).     This section shows the results: strategy candidate groups and the prioritized list driven by the weights.

    Use the ▸ arrow on a group to filter to specific outcomes, and use the table toggle to switch between values and scores for each metric column.     Select a school to view its decision flowchart. The flowchart shows how the thresholds lead to the recommended strategy group for that school. Active decision nodes and threshold values are highlighted.
