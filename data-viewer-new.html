📊 Jeffco Dashboard Data & Logic

Comprehensive view of all data sources, calculations, and decision logic

📋 Dashboard Overview

The Jeffco Dashboard uses multiple data sources to evaluate schools and provide strategic decision recommendations for Jefferson County School District.

🎯 Primary Data Sources

  • Decision Data Export.csv - Contains evaluation metrics, growth data, and building scores
  • Schools.geojson - Contains geographic data and school locations for mapping
  • Map_Export.csv - Contains additional geographic and capacity data

📧 Key Features

  • School Decision Evaluation - Evaluates schools based on multiple criteria
  • Interactive Map - Visualizes school locations and decision categories
  • Interactive Flowchart - Visualizes decision logic for individual schools
  • Flow-based Filtering - Filter schools by decision flow categories
  • Geographic Analysis - Maps schools and analyzes spatial relationships

📊 Decision Categories

Building Addition
Schools that could expand on their current site
Policy Change & Re-sort
Schools requiring policy adjustments and student reassignment
Replacement
Schools needing complete replacement
Building Addition & Major Capital
Schools needing both expansion and major capital investment
Target Capital Investment
Schools requiring targeted capital improvements
Standard Maintenance
Schools needing routine maintenance
Major Capital Investment
Schools requiring significant capital improvements
Consolidation (Welcoming)
Welcoming schools that receive students from other schools
Consolidation with Capital
Schools to be consolidated with capital improvements
Closure (Goes Into Welcoming)
Schools to be closed and students moved to welcoming schools
Closure & Replacement
Schools to be closed and replaced
Other / Unknown
Schools requiring further evaluation

🔧 Decision Logic Flow

The dashboard uses a multi-step evaluation process to determine the appropriate action for each school.

🗺️ 4-Flow Decision Structure

Flow 1: Decision Routing

Main routing based on utilization and growth patterns

  • Check utilization against thresholds
  • Evaluate enrollment growth trends
  • Assess distance to underutilized schools
  • Route to appropriate sub-flow (2, 3, or 4)

Flow 2: Building Addition (Expansion)

Schools that could expand on their current site

  • Check site capacity for expansion
  • Evaluate building quality
  • Assess program adequacy
  • Determine if expansion is feasible

Flow 3: Maintenance/Investment

Schools needing facility or program improvements

  • Evaluate building condition scores
  • Check program offerings
  • Determine investment priority
  • Classify as maintenance or major investment

Flow 4: Consolidation/Closure

Schools recommended for consolidation or closure

  • Check recent investment status
  • Evaluate program adequacy
  • Assess composite building score
  • Check distance to welcoming schools (varies by school level)
  • Determine consolidation or closure path

🎯 Decision Categories Explained

🟢 Expansion Category

  • Building Addition - Schools that can expand on-site
  • Replacement - Schools needing complete replacement
  • Building Addition & Major Capital - Expansion with major investment

🟠 Maintenance/Investment Category

  • Target Capital Investment - Targeted improvements
  • Standard Maintenance - Routine maintenance needs
  • Major Capital Investment - Significant improvements

🗺️ Closure/Consolidation Category

  • Consolidation (Welcoming) - Welcoming schools that receive students
  • Consolidation with Capital - Merge with improvements
  • Closure (Goes Into Welcoming) - Close and reassign students to welcoming schools
  • Closure & Replacement - Close and replace

⚪ Other Category

  • Policy Change & Re-sort - Policy adjustments needed
  • Other / Unknown - Requires further evaluation

🧮 Formulas & Calculations

Detailed explanations of all calculations and thresholds used in the decision logic.

🗺️ Utilization Rate

Utilization = Current Enrollment / Capacity

Threshold: 60% (default)
High Utilization Threshold: 100% (default)
Purpose: Identifies underutilized and overcrowded schools

📊 Enrollment Growth (2014-2024)

Growth Rate = (2024 Enrollment - 2014 Enrollment) / 2014 Enrollment

Threshold: 0% (default)
Purpose: Identifies schools with declining enrollment trends

??????? Building Quality Score

Lower scores = Better building condition

Threshold: 5 (1-10 scale, default)
Purpose: Identifies schools needing facility improvements (lower scores = better condition)

🗺️ Distance to Underutilized Schools

Distance in miles to nearest underutilized school

Threshold: 3.5 miles (default)
Purpose: Identifies schools that could serve students from nearby underutilized schools

🗺️ Educational Adequacy

Count of academic and extracurricular programs

Threshold: 2 programs (default)
Purpose: Identifies schools needing program enhancements

?? Adjustable Parameters

All thresholds can be adjusted using the sliders in the dashboard:

  • Enrollment Thresholds by School Level:
    • Elementary School: 100-500 students (default: 240)
    • K-8 School: 200-600 students (default: 360)
    • Middle School: 300-800 students (default: 500)
    • High School: 400-1200 students (default: 700)
    • 6-12 School: 300-1000 students (default: 600)
  • Utilization Threshold: 0-100% (default: 60%)
  • High Utilization Threshold: 0-120% (default: 100%)
  • Enrollment Growth Threshold: -50% to +50% (default: 0%)
  • Distance Threshold: 0-5 miles (default: 3.5)
  • Composite Building Score: 1-10 scale (default: 5)
  • Educational Adequacy Minimum: 0-10 programs (default: 2)
  • Recent Investment Threshold: 0-20 years (default: 5)
  • Distance to Welcoming Schools (by school level):
    • Elementary School: 1-10 miles (default: 4.0)
    • K-8 School: 1-10 miles (default: 6.0)
    • Middle School: 1-10 miles (default: 6.0)
    • High School: 1-10 miles (default: 6.0)
    • 6-12 School: 1-10 miles (default: 6.0)

🎯 Flow-based Filtering

The dashboard includes flow-based filtering that groups decision types into logical categories:

  • Expansion: Building Addition, Replacement, Building Addition & Major Capital
  • Maintenance/Investment: Target Capital Investment, Standard Maintenance, Major Capital Investment
  • Closure/Consolidation: Consolidation (Goes Into Welcoming), Consolidation with Capital, Closure (Goes Into Welcoming), Closure & Replacement
  • Other: Policy Change & Re-sort, Other / Unknown
