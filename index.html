How to use the JeffCo Planning Tool

JeffCo Facility Planning Dashboard

This tool is intended to support JeffCo facility planning initiatives. Users can explore conditions data and model school choice scenarios to better understand the potential impact of facility decisions.

📊 Data & Logic
School Decision Evaluation Use the sliders to adjust how schools are evaluated across multiple factors, which is reflected in School Decision Evaluation: Results on the right sidebar.
The flowchart allows you to visualize the decision logic dynamically by school.

  • Enrollment
    Minimum number of enrolled students
  • Utilization
    Sets the minimum percentage of a school's capacity that should be used before it's considered underutilized.
  • High Utilization
    Defines when a school is considered highly utilized or potentially overcrowded.
  • Enrollment Growth
    Minimum expected change in student enrollment (as a percentage).
  • Projected Utilization
    Target for future utilization based on projected enrollment.
  • Distance to Underutilized Schools
    Maximum distance to look for nearby underutilized schools for reassignment.
  • Building Quality
    Minimum required building condition score.
  • Educational Adequacy Minimum
    Minimum percentage threshold for educational adequacy (0-100%).

    How to interpret the outcomes

    School & Student Decisions

    In this section you see the results of your choices. It has two key functions:

    • Evaluation Results: When you adjust the evaluation sliders on the left (like utilization or building condition), this section instantly updates to show recommended actions for each school—both in summary and detailed views.
    • Scenario Impact Analysis: After running a simulation (like closing a school), this section displays the effects—such as where students will go, how enrollment shifts, and how travel distances change.
    School Decision Evaluation: Results This section shows the results of the school decision evaluation based on your selected criteria.
    Summary Table
    This table provides a summary of recommended actions for all schools based on your selected evaluation criteria.
    Decision by School
    This table lists the recommended action for each individual school, allowing you to review and compare decisions at the school level.
    Model Output: Impact Analysis This section displays the impact analysis of your scenario, including enrollment, utilization, and travel distance changes.
