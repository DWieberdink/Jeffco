    How to interpret the outcomes

    School & Student Decisions

    In this section you see the results of your choices. It has two key functions:

    • Evaluation Results: When you adjust the evaluation sliders on the left (like utilization or building condition), this section instantly updates to show recommended actions for each school—both in summary and detailed views.
    • Scenario Impact Analysis: After running a simulation (like closing a school), this section displays the effects—such as where students will go, how enrollment shifts, and how travel distances change.
    Strategic Sorting: Results
    Summary Table
    This table shows the count of schools in each strategy group (decision type) based on the Strategic Sorting thresholds. Strategy groups are dynamically generated from the decision outcomes.
    Decision by School
    This table lists each school with its assigned decision type (strategy group) based on the Strategic Sorting thresholds. Schools are grouped by their decision outcome.
    Model Output: Impact Analysis

    Strategy Candidate Groups

    This section allows you to prioritize schools within each strategy group based on customizable criteria. Select a strategy group using the tabs below, then adjust the weight sliders to reflect your priorities. Schools will be ranked based on your weighting, with higher priority schools appearing at the top of the list and as larger circles on the map.

    Strategic Sorting uses decision thresholds to categorize schools into strategy groups. Adjust the sliders below to change how schools are evaluated, which is reflected in Strategic Sorting: Results on the right sidebar.
    The flowchart allows you to visualize the decision logic dynamically by school.

    Flow 1: Decision Routing
    • Enrollment Thresholds by School Level
      Minimum enrollment thresholds for Elementary, K-8, Middle, High, and 6-12 schools (driven by Decision Data Export enrollment)
    • Current Utilization Threshold
      Minimum percentage of capacity used before a school is considered underutilized (0-100%, default: 60%)
    • High Utilization Threshold
      Percentage threshold for identifying highly utilized or overcrowded schools (0-120%, default: 100%)
    • Enrollment Growth Threshold
      Minimum expected change in student enrollment (-50% to +50%, default: 0%)
    • Distance to Nearby Schools by School Level
      Distance thresholds (miles, varies by school level) used to determine whether nearby schools are within a “reasonable” distance
    Flow 2: Expansion
    • Attendance Area Enrollment
      Percentage threshold for attendance area enrollment (60-100%, default: 80%)
    • Composite Building Score
      Building condition score threshold (1-10 scale, default: 5)
    • Educational Adequacy
      Minimum percentage threshold for educational adequacy (0-100%, default: 50%)
    Flow 3: Maintenance/Investment
    • Composite Building Score Above/Below Threshold
      Building condition score thresholds (1-10 scale, default: 5)
    Flow 4: Closure/Consolidation
    • Composite Building Score
      Building condition score threshold (1-10 scale, default: 5)
    • Distance to Welcoming Schools
      Distance thresholds by school level (miles)
     Prioritization within Strategy Sorting lets you prioritize schools within a strategy group using weighted criteria.

    Controls panel (Left):
    Use the weight sliders (0–10, 0.5 increments). You can also disable a metric using its checkbox. Higher weights increase that metric’s influence on the priority score.

    Results panel (Right):
    Select one or more strategy groups to generate a prioritized list. Each group row has a ▸ arrow that opens a flyout where you can optionally filter to specific outcomes within that group.

    Table display:
    Use the “Show values / Show scores” toggle above the table to switch between the raw values (%, miles, counts) and the normalized 0–100 metric scores. This toggle does not change the ranking.
